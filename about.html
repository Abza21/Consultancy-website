I build tools that make spatial data useful.

I'm Goitsemodimo Abel Senosi, a GIS and software consultant based in Pretoria, South Africa. I help organisations analyse, visualise, and operationalise their spatial data through custom web applications and dashboards.

Short bio

I work at the intersection of spatial science and modern web engineering. That means I don't just produce maps, I build the dashboards, web applications, and tools that make spatial data actionable for the people who need it.

My background combines a BSc in Biology & Geography, a BSc Honours in Geography specialising in GIS and Environmental Assessment, and over five years of freelance software development experience. This dual skill set lets me take a project from raw satellite imagery and GPS data all the way through to a live, interactive web dashboard.

I've applied these skills to environmental impact assessments, mining and water-quality research, CO₂ emissions analysis, utility monitoring dashboards, and business websites. Whether the problem is spatial or digital, my approach is the same: understand the question, choose the right tools, and deliver something that works.

What I do

🌍 GIS & Spatial Analysis

I analyse spatial data to answer real-world questions how mining affects water quality, how land use changes over time, where environmental risks are concentrated. I work with QGIS, ArcGIS, remote sensing data, and Python to produce decision-support maps and technical reports.

💻 Web & Software Development

I build responsive websites, CMS-driven platforms, and Python desktop applications for businesses that need custom digital tools. My stack includes Python, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Sanity CMS, and API integrations.

📊 Geospatial Dashboards

I design and build real-time web GIS dashboards like GridSense, a utility monitoring system that visualises live electrical faults, integrates mobile app incident reports, and automates field technician dispatch. Built with Python, PostGIS, and Leaflet.
