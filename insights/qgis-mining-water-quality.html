Using QGIS to Assess Mining Impacts on Water Quality

2026-09-20, 1 min read

Mapping water-quality samples against mining sites shows patterns a table cannot.

Steps

  • Georeference your sample points.
  • Add mining boundaries and rivers as layers.
  • Measure distances and compare results across zones.
  • Produce a thematic map that a non-specialist can read.
