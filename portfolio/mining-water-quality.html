GIS-Based Assessment of Mining Impacts on Water Quality (Rustenburg)

Honours research mapping how mining affects water quality.

Problem

Mining activity around Rustenburg may be degrading water quality, and the spatial pattern was not clearly mapped.

Solution

Used GIS and spatial analysis to relate water-quality data to mining locations and produced thematic maps for the findings.

Result

A research-backed, map-driven view of mining impacts on water quality.

Tools

QGISArcGISSatellite imagery
